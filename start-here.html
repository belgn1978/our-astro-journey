Fast route for this week

  1. Spend 10 minutes on weather and choose one likely clear evening.
  2. Pick one easy target (moon, bright cluster, or visual meteor watch).
  3. Follow the first-night checklist exactly once without adding extras.
  4. Compare your result with examples in gallery pages.
  5. Write one note about what worked and what to change next time.
