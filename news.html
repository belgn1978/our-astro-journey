How to use this event calendar

This page combines a focused calendar of major crewed and flagship launches, eclipses, meteor showers, notable comet opportunities, and deep-sky observing seasons with relevant astronomy and astrophotography updates. Use it to shortlist events, then check cloud forecasts and light-pollution conditions before you commit to a session.

For solar events, always review eye and equipment safety in our solar eclipse safety guide.

For meteor showers, prioritize dark locations and visual observing comfort. For lunar events, even short cloud breaks can still be useful for practice shots and focus drills.

Topics include lunar eclipse viewing, safe solar eclipse imaging, meteor shower peaks, Milky Way and galaxy season planning, deep-sky observing, and major crewed spaceflight events. Choose your hemisphere to see the targets most useful from your part of the world.

Beginners often benefit from keeping a simple observation log with date, conditions, target, and outcome. Over a few months, this creates a practical guide tailored to your own location and equipment.
