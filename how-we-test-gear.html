How We Test Astronomy Gear

This page explains our testing method so beginners can trust what we publish and understand the context behind each recommendation.

Visit reviews hub Start here

Testing principles

Beginner-first scoring

We prioritize what matters most to newcomers: setup clarity, reliability, app usability, realistic results, and value.

  • Setup time and ease.
  • Connection and app stability.
  • First-night success rate.
  • Output quality in ordinary UK conditions.
  • Value for beginner budgets.

Real-world test context

We do not test only in perfect skies. Sessions include mixed cloud, light pollution, and short time windows because that is real beginner life.

  • Local sky quality and weather conditions are stated.
  • Target type and session length are documented.
  • Any failed attempts are noted, not hidden.

Disclosure and independence

  • We clearly mark affiliate relationships.
  • Sponsored products do not guarantee positive opinions.
  • We explicitly include limits, tradeoffs, and who should avoid a product.

See discounts and affiliate links for current offers and disclosure context.

How to use our reviews

  • Match review context to your own sky and schedule.
  • Start with one clear use case rather than all-purpose expectations.
  • Pair review reading with weather and dark sky planning.
