About Our Astrophotography Journey

We are learning by doing, sharing beginner-friendly guidance about moon photography, deep-sky practice, and the joy of exploring the night sky.

My name is Matt and my daughter’s name is Lacey, and this is the story of our journey into beginner astrophotography.

Neither of us had a background in astrophotography to begin with, but I have developed a growing fascination with the night sky and Lacey is already studying photography at school.

We are learning by starting with a mobile phone, keeping costs low, and practicing from a light-polluted back garden while exploring moon photography, nebula imaging, galaxy photography, and image processing techniques.

We also share weather forecasts, dark sky site suggestions, and hands-on learning as we improve our skills over time.

As we test new approaches, we focus on practical steps that other beginners can copy without specialist jargon. If a method is confusing, expensive, or unrealistic for first-time observers, we say so clearly.

We are also building practical coverage for smart telescope users, including DWARF Mini, DWARF 3, and Seestar-related topics, so newcomers can compare options with honest expectations.

Our goal is simple: help beginners enjoy the sky more often by combining plain-English guides, real-world image attempts, and transparent review notes.
