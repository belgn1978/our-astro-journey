DwarfLabs Dwarf Mini Review

What It Is

The Dwarf mini is a small smart telescope. It can be used to photograph and video in multiple formats while controlling everything from your phone or tablet.

This allows you to capture different things from birds in a tree to another galaxy.

The Dwarf mini also processes the final image for solar system and deep sky objects for you and you can then download the dinal image to you device ready to share.

The app itself has lots of great features where you get to be a part of the comunity.

Specs and they mean for you

Dimenshions
140mm Height, 115mm width and 45mm depth
The Dwarf Mini is about the size of a thick paperback book, making it easy to carry in a backpack or take on a holiday.
Weight
1.3kg
About the same weight as a 1.25l bottle of water. Light enough to carry comfortably but heavy enough to feel sturdy.
Aperture
50mm
This is the size of the front lense. Think of it as the telescopes light bucket. A larger aperture collects more light, helping you capture fainter targets.
Focal Length
100mm
This determines how "zoomed in" the view is. Short focal lengths provide a wider view but targets can look smaller, while longer focal lengths gives you a more zoomed in view. This can make it harder to find and track.
Battery
Built in rechareable battery via USB-C (up to 4 hours)
Long enough for most observing sessions but always have a fully charged power bank handy.
Storage
64GB Internal storage
Plenty of space for multiple sessions but the Dwarf Mini has to option for memory upgrades such as a memory card, so you will need to move some data via the included usb-c cable to a computer.
Filters
Solar Filter, Dual Band Filter, Astro Filter
The included Solar Filter is required to safely observe the sun. The Duel Band Filter is best for nebulae. The Astro Filter is the general purpose filter for everything else.
