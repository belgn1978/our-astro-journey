Why dark skies matter

If you are new to astrophotography, moving to a dark-sky location often improves results more than buying expensive equipment. Less light pollution and sky glow means better contrast in deep-sky photography and easier target detection.

For moon photography and bright targets you can still practice from home, but this page helps you find alternatives when you want to push further.

Build a simple planning routine

  • Pick two possible observing spots here, then compare their conditions on the weather page.
  • Use the news and sky events page to match location planning with upcoming targets.
  • Use useful resources for software and maps that help with field planning.

As your confidence grows, repeat this process for a few different sites. Over time you will build a short list of reliable locations for moon work, wide-field practice, and deep-sky sessions.
