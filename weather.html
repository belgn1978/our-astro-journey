How beginners can use this page

Cloud cover tells you whether the sky is likely to be visible. Transparency matters for faint deep-sky targets, seeing affects how steady fine detail appears, and wind can shake a tripod or telescope. Temperature, humidity, and dew risk can also influence how comfortably and reliably you can observe.

A short planning routine helps: check cloud cover and the forecast conditions, compare two nearby locations, then decide whether moon photography or deep-sky photography is realistic for that night. If conditions are poor, use the session for equipment setup practice instead of a long capture attempt.
